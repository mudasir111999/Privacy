1. Description of Service

Autofill Vault is a browser extension that lets you securely store login entries locally in your browser and autofill login forms on supported websites. It also includes a specialised workflow for the Pakistani FBR IRIS portal (iris.fbr.gov.pk) where CNIC, mobile number, and email-based autofill are optimised for that site.

Important: Your login entries — usernames, passwords, notes, mobile numbers, CNICs, and any IRIS-specific fields — are stored locally on your device using chrome.storage.local and are never transmitted to our servers.

2. License and Activation

The Extension offers a free tier and two paid plans — PRO (time-limited) and MAX (lifetime).

  • The free version is limited to a small, fixed number of saved accounts (currently 4 entries). To store more, a valid license key is required.
  • PRO licenses are valid for a fixed period (typically one year from the date of activation, unless stated otherwise on purchase).
  • MAX licenses are lifetime — they do not expire.
  • Each license key supports a configurable number of device slots. A new device automatically claims a free slot; when all slots are in use, the least-recently-used slot may be reclaimed by the publisher on request.
  • License keys may be reset, reassigned, or revoked at the publisher's discretion in cases of abuse or fraudulent activity.

Privacy Policy

Autofill Vault is a Chrome extension that stores login entries locally in your browser and can autofill login forms.

What data is stored

Locally (in your browser): any login entries you create in the extension, including the record's label, username, password, notes / mobile, site domain, and — when you are on the FBR IRIS portal — the optional email and email password fields. Entries are saved using chrome.storage.local, which scopes them to your browser profile on this device.
Alongside your entries, the extension also stores a locally generated install ID (a random UUID), your activated license key (if any), and small UI preferences such as the "Show All Websites" toggle state.
Not stored on our server: your saved usernames, passwords, notes, emails, and any other entry content are never uploaded. They never leave your device through this extension.

License verification

To unlock PRO or MAX features, the extension verifies your license with our license server. During verification, the extension sends only:

  • License key — the key you entered to activate.
  • Install ID — a random UUID generated the first time the extension runs on your browser. It is not tied to your name, email, or any other personal identifier.

We use this information only to validate licenses, track which devices have claimed a slot on a multi-device key, and enforce the per-key device limit. The server returns the license status (valid / expired / disabled), the plan (PRO or MAX), the expiry date (if any), and the number of device slots in use.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect browsing history.
  • We do not collect keystrokes.
  • We do not collect or store your saved passwords on our server.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data.

Third-party services

The extension may open an external link (for example, a WhatsApp chat) if you choose to purchase a license. WhatsApp is a third-party service and is governed by its own privacy policy.

Data deletion

You have several ways to remove your data:

  • Use the "Clear All" button inside the extension to wipe every entry saved for the current site.
  • Delete individual entries with the trash icon next to each record.
  • Uninstall the extension from chrome://extensions — this removes all locally stored data, including entries, install ID, and license key.

To release a device slot on a multi-device license (for example, when replacing a computer), contact us via WhatsApp with your license key and we can free the slot on our side.
